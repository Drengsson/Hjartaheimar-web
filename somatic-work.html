Private sessions

Coming Home to Yourself

We can know where our feelings come from and have talked about them, yet still feel tension or unease that's hard to put into words. What happens when we slow down and start listening to the body?

In these private sessions I bring together somatic bodywork, emotional expression, breath, deep meditation and healing. No session is planned in advance. Each one is shaped by you, how you feel and what comes up.

Connecting with the body

We slow down, connect with the breath and notice where tension and feelings sit. You don't need to understand everything. Sometimes it's enough to pay attention.

Giving feelings room

Movement, sound, tears or laughter. We can also use cushions to squeeze, scream into or throw safely. Sometimes the expression is powerful, sometimes soft, and sometimes stillness is what's needed. There's no expectation of any particular experience.

Deep meditation

Through guided meditation we explore how feelings show up in the body. Do they have a shape, a colour, a movement? Sometimes images or memories come up, sometimes simply calm.

Healing and energy work

As you wish, Reiki, sound and consented touch are woven in, and sometimes Light Language, spontaneous expression through voice and tones. This part is always optional.

Coming back to yourself

At the end we take plenty of time to rest and let the experience settle.

To feel. To express. To allow. To connect.
