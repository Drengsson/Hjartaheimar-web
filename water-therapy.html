Flæði and Aguahara · Álftaneslaug

Water Therapy

Let the water carry you.

Warm water makes room to slow down and let go. The water surrounds you and carries you, and with gentle movement and breath the body can relax and the mind can rest. You don't have to hold anything together. The water does that.

Group sessions · every other Thursday at 20:00 Private sessions · same evenings at 19:00

Group sessions · Flæði

Together in the water

In Flæði we come together in warm water and make room for presence, trust and connection. Through breath, gentle movement and free flow, the body finds its own rhythm.

Each participant also receives their own treatment during the session, with the body supported and gently guided through movement, stretches and flow. If you wish, part of the treatment can take place beneath the surface.

No previous experience is needed. You experience Flæði on your own terms.

Private sessions · Aguahara

All the attention on you

In a private session the time is all yours. With gentle support, movement and stretches, the body gets to rest in the water. You don't need to do anything but receive.

The treatment is shaped around you and can be both deeply relaxing and nourishing. Part of it can take place beneath the surface, with a nose clip and support the whole time. You always decide how far you go.

All you have to do is float.

When

Every other Thursday at 19:00, before Flæði

Length and price

60 minutes · ISK 15,000
