About Gulla

Guðlaug Ósk Pétursdóttir

I'm a yoga teacher, goldsmith, and a leisure and social studies specialist. Beneath all these roles lies the same curiosity about the connection between body, mind, emotions and energy.

Hjartaheimar grew out of a wish to create a space where nothing needs fixing or rushing, just room to give ourselves time to be.
