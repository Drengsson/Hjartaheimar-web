Yoga Nidra for Companies

Give your team room to slow down.

In the rush of the working day it is easy to forget to pause, breathe and rest. Yoga Nidra is a guided deep relaxation that gives your team a chance to step out of the pace, release tension and reconnect with themselves. No movement and no previous experience of yoga is needed.

What is Yoga Nidra?

Guided deep relaxation, lying down and without effort

Yoga Nidra takes place lying down in a comfortable position. With calm guidance, attention is drawn to the breath, the body and the inner experience. Participants do not have to do anything but lie still and listen.

This is not a yoga class in the usual sense. Nobody needs to move, change clothes or know anything beforehand, which makes it suitable for any group whatever their age or fitness.

A moment to pause. A moment to breathe. A moment to rest.

The session can help your team to:

  • Ease stress and physical tension
  • Support rest and recovery
  • Quieten the mind and improve focus
  • Build body awareness and a sense of their own wellbeing
  • Create more calm and balance in everyday life

How the session works

I come to you

I come to your workplace and lead your team through Yoga Nidra. Participants lie comfortably on mats, usually with a blanket, a pillow and an eye pillow, while I guide them through breath, body awareness and deep relaxation.

Very little is needed. A quiet room that can be closed off, and space for the group to lie down. If there are not mats for everyone, the session works just as well on a carpet with a blanket, or sitting in a chair. People stay in their own clothes throughout and need to bring nothing.

I will advise you on equipment and where to find it. The sessions suit small and large groups alike and are adapted to each workplace.

Where

At your workplace, in a meeting room, canteen or any other space that can be closed off.

When

At lunchtime, in the middle of the day or at the end of it. We find a time that disrupts the day as little as possible.

Who it is for

Everyone, whatever their age, fitness or experience. Nobody has to move or know anything in advance.

What is needed

A quiet room and space to lie down. Mats, blankets and pillows if you have them, otherwise a blanket is enough.
