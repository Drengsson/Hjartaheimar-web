Reiki Healing

A moment to receive.

Reiki is a Japanese energy treatment built on the idea that life energy flows through everything living. You lie on a table in a calm setting while hands are placed lightly on the body, or held just above it.

Request a time

How the treatment works

The practitioner works through a set flow, directing attention to different areas of the body. Many people feel warmth, tingling, lightness or deep stillness during the treatment.

For some the mind grows quieter, while others feel a stronger connection to the body, or find that feelings that have been sitting under the surface get some room. The experience is personal, and no two treatments are the same.

Reiki is used to support balance, wellbeing and relaxation. It's a beautiful way to give yourself time to stop, listen inward and notice what the body and mind are saying. All you need to do is come as you are and allow yourself to receive.
